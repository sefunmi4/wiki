# Entity Governance & Legal Standing

## Overview
This document outlines the foundational legal, corporate, and entity-level compliance standards for **Architecture of Homo Sapiens LLC** and its registered assets.

## Registration & Jurisdiction
* **Legal Registration:** Registered as a Limited Liability Company in the State of New Jersey and with the IRS.
* **Corporate Scope:** Acts as the primary holding entity and legal umbrella for intellectual property, brand assets, software platforms, and subsidiary operations (including *Architecture of Ashiru* and *SK Network*).

## Operational Compliance Pillars
1. **Financial Segregation:** Utilizing dedicated sub-accounts (such as Relay Financial) to strictly isolate operating expenses, label revenues, and project-specific capital.
2. **Asset Protection:** Maintaining clear distinction between personal holdings and corporate assets to ensure good standing under local and federal business laws.
