# Operational Workflow: Financials & Label Management

## Overview
Operational protocols for managing Architecture of Homo Sapiens LLC accounts, banking segregation, operational burn rate tracking, and music distribution.

## Key Operational Procedures
1. **Relay Financial Management:** Maintain isolated sub-accounts (spanning operational, expense, tax, reserve, and label silos) for distinct brand assets, expenses, and label payouts. Use exported CSV or PDF statements for periodic gross and net burn rate calculations and accounting book updates.
2. **DistroKid Administration:** Utilize the Ultimate (Label) Plan to manage multi-artist distribution under the *SK Network* banner while maintaining custom label naming rights.

## Current Financial Baseline & Metrics
* **Core Operating Inflows:** Capital tracking via dedicated operating sub-accounts.
* **Key Operating Expenses:** Infrastructure and tooling stack (including cloud computing, communication APIs, and development subscriptions).
* **Burn Rate Monitoring:** Regular reconciliation of gross and net burn rates across active accounts to ensure fiscal health and long-term sustainability.
