# Operational Workflow: Financials & Label Management

## Overview
Operational protocols for managing Architecture of Homo Sapiens LLC accounts, banking segregation, and music distribution.

## Key Operational Procedures
1. **Relay Financial Management:** Maintain isolated sub-accounts for distinct brand assets, expenses, and label payouts. Use exported CSV or PDF statements for periodic gross and net burn rate calculations.
2. **DistroKid Administration:** Utilize the Ultimate (Label) Plan to manage multi-artist distribution under the *SK Network* banner while maintaining custom label naming rights.
