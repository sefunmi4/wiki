# Global Strategy & International Compliance Framework

## Overview
This document defines the overarching operational, international, and cross-border legal strategy for **Architecture of Homo Sapiens LLC** and all subsidiary projects (*E-CO*, *Ethos*, *ECHO*, *LOVE*, and *SK Network*).

## Global Operating Standards
1. **Multinational Architecture Model:** Structuring international compliance and legal operations with high-level rigor (similar to major enterprise practices utilizing specialized hubs like London legal frameworks for English-speaking international jurisdictions).
2. **Regional Adaptability:** Ensuring that business, platform, and brand practices respect, adapt to, and comply with local statutory laws in every region of operation.
3. **Multi-Language Integrity:** Prioritizing accurate legal, technical, and cultural translation across all terms of service, agreements, and brand documentation for non-English speaking markets.
